How to fly the Wolfram

Controls

WASD move · mouse look (click to capture) · Shift run
Zero-g (drive off): you float. WASD push you where you look, Space/C up/down; let go near a wall to grab a handrail. Under thrust the floors are down and you walk. On EVA the same keys fly your jet pack.
E use console (the view locks onto its screen; Exit / Esc to leave) / pick up / lift · Esc menu
R on a faulty module opens its repair procedure: a workbench where you take it apart with real tools (1–8 pick a tool, B change bit/socket, hold the mouse to work). Each fault needs specific parts from stores (circuit cards, wiring, seals, bearings, HV cells, filler rod, optics), so keep the Cargo deck stocked. On a module with no fault, hold R for routine maintenance.
F (hold) use your item: extinguisher, hull patch, medkit, cartridge
Maintenance tablet (any emergency locker): F opens a 3D wireframe of the ship with every module's state; drag to turn, wheel to zoom, tap a module for its fault and parts; 1–4 ship / crew / nav / stores
Ladder: under thrust, walk into the hazard-striped gap in the trunk railing; W/S climb (look down to go down), A/D step off at a deck
Sleep: E at a bunk (Habitation sleep stations, or the ring bunks for the best rest); E/Space wakes you
Work pod (EVA, beside the hatch): E board · F grapple / release cargo · G exit · R repairs from 8 m
Dead? V picks a ghost job: free camera, scout drone, or the external camera net ([ ])
G drop item · Tab (hold) ship status · T chat · 1–4 vote · M mute

iPad / tablets: left stick to move, drag the right side to look. USE / REPAIR / ITEM buttons act on whatever is in front of you. With a Magic Keyboard, trackpad or mouse, just use it: the game switches to keyboard & mouse controls and clicking the view locks the pointer (or tap MOUSE). Touching the screen brings the touch controls back.

The ship (bottom to top)

Deck 0 Drive: torch drive, drive control, reaction-mass tanks, pellet injector.
Deck 1 Cargo: fabricator, stores (spare kits!), drone bay (external repairs), torpedo magazine, starboard airlock.
Deck 2 Engineering: fusion reactor, power distribution, battery, thermal control, coolant pumps.
Deck 3 Habitation: atmosphere control, CO₂ scrubbers, water, hydroponics, medbay, cryo bay, storm shelter, centrifuge control.
Centrifuge ring: reached from the lift (via the hub and a spoke ladder). Spin it up at Centrifuge Control for 0.17–0.5 g; twelve empty science bays.
Deck 4 Bridge: helm, navigation, sensors, fire control, comms, status board.
An open trunk runs through the centre of every deck: float through it in zero-g, or ride the platform lift under thrust. Each deck has a red emergency locker (suits, extinguishers, patches, medkits).
Exterior (EVA): suit up, then use the airlock on the Cargo deck. Fly the jet pack on a 110 m tether to repair radiators, gatling PDCs, the missile launcher, the railgun, antenna and sensors by hand. Any thrust reels you in.

Getting started

1. Take the lift to the Bridge, open the Helm, press Undock & depart.
2. On easy difficulties, engage the autopilot. Otherwise ignite, set thrust, and watch the "flip in…" countdown: burn toward the target, coast, flip 180° and burn to stop.
3. Watch the HUD. Red alert means something needs a human.

Survival tips

• Heat is the enemy in space. Radiators dump it, and combat or railgun shots fill the heat sink.
• Above 2.2 g, crew who aren't sitting at a console (acceleration couch) get hurt.
• Hull breaches vent a deck. Grab a suit and a patch, and hold F on the breach. Venting a deck on purpose kills fires.
• Solar storm warning? Everyone into the blue storm shelter on Habitation deck.
• Destroyed components need a Spare Parts Kit from Stores (Cargo deck). External parts can be fixed by drones, or by hand on an EVA. Keep thrust under 0.6 g while anyone is outside, and watch your suit O₂.
• When you die, a backup crew member thaws from cryo. Keep the cryo bay powered.
• Docking at a station refuels you and gives credits to spend at Comms.
